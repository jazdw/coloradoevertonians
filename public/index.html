Who we are

Colorado Evertonians started the way so many supporters clubs do: a couple of Blues bumped into each other at a soccer bar and set up a Facebook group to find more. Today we're a friendly, diverse bunch of Evertonians living all over the state. Some were born into it, some found the club later, and everyone is welcome.

We hold regular match watch parties in the Denver area, and elsewhere in Colorado when there's demand.

Where we watch

We meet for every Everton game at BARna, a soccer bar on East Colfax in Capitol Hill. Early kick-offs, late kick-offs, derby days: look for the group in blue.
